Table: CUSTOMER
Description: Stores information about customers who are eligible for offers or have made purchases. Each customer has a unique ID and basic profile data.

customer_id: Unique identifier for the customer (UUID).

full_name: Full name of the customer.

email: Email address of the customer. Must be unique.

phone_number: Optional phone number for contacting the customer.

address: Full physical mailing address of the customer.

customer_segment: Category or classification of the customer (e.g., Retail, Wholesale, VIP).

created_at: Timestamp indicating when the customer record was created.

updated_at: Timestamp indicating when the customer record was last modified.


Table: PRODUCT
Description: Contains information about products that can have offers applied to them. Each product is uniquely identified and categorized.

product_id: Unique identifier for the product (UUID).

product_name: Human-readable name of the product.

sku: Unique stock keeping unit assigned to the product.

category: Category under which the product is classified (e.g., Electronics, Apparel).

price: Standard unit price of the product.

description: Long-form textual description of the product.

available: Boolean flag indicating whether the product is currently available for purchase.

created_at: Timestamp when the product was added to the system.

updated_at: Timestamp of the most recent update to the product record.



Table: OFFER
Description: Tracks promotional offers that apply to specific products and may optionally be personalized for customers. Includes details about offer type, discount value, and validity window.

offer_id: Unique identifier for the offer (UUID).

offer_title: Title or short label for the offer.

offer_description: Long-form description providing context and details about the offer.

offer_type: Classification of the offer (e.g., Discount, Bundle, BOGO).

discount_percentage: Percentage-based discount applied to the product, if applicable.

fixed_discount_amount: Fixed currency amount discount, if applicable.

effective_date: Date on which the offer becomes active and can be used.

expiration_date: Date on which the offer expires and is no longer valid.

status: Current status of the offer (e.g., Active, Expired, Scheduled).

customer_id: ID of the customer this offer is targeted toward. Nullable if offer is general.

product_id: ID of the product to which the offer applies.

created_at: Timestamp when the offer was created.

updated_at: Timestamp of the latest update to the offer record.
